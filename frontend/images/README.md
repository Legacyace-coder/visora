# Visora website image assets

Place the following image files directly inside this `images` folder.
Use the exact filenames shown below (lowercase, hyphen-separated).

## Public pages

- `home-hero-editorial.jpg` — wide editorial hero photograph; architecture, landscape or a striking visual scene.
- `home-story-photographer.jpg` — photographer actively making an image.
- `home-portfolio-feature.jpg` — fine-art or editorial photography portfolio / print.
- `home-submission-feature.jpg` — contact sheets, image review or curated photo selection.
- `home-publishing-feature.jpg` — open photography book, magazine or printed editorial spread.
- `about-hero.jpg` — atmospheric editorial photograph for the About page.
- `about-photographer.jpg` — photographer reviewing or editing photographs.
- `collection-editorial.jpg` — a strong standalone editorial photograph for the Collection page.
- `photographers-hero.jpg` — photographer working with a camera, ideally in natural light.
- `photographers-workspace.jpg` — camera, prints and tools arranged in a creative workspace.

## Sign-in and registration backgrounds

- `login-background.jpg` — subtle, atmospheric photography / camera detail; landscape orientation.
- `register-background.jpg` — photography studio or creative workspace; landscape orientation.

## Account workspaces

- `photographer-workspace.jpg` — camera and printed photographs in a photographer's workspace.
- `agent-workspace.jpg` — editorial desk, photography briefs or visual research.
- `admin-workspace.jpg` — photography archive, print review or editorial workspace.

## Image recommendations

- Prefer original images you own or have a clear commercial-use licence to use.
- Avoid watermarked images and images with visible third-party logos.
- Use JPG for photographs; aim for 1600–2400 px on the long edge.
- Compress images for the web (ideally around 200–500 KB each where quality permits).
- Keep the photographer-submitted portfolio images out of this folder. Those remain user uploads.
- Public-page slides use these assets as editorial imagery, not as published gallery works. Verify ownership and commercial-use rights for each image before deployment.

Missing files will show a designed neutral placeholder until you add the corresponding image.
