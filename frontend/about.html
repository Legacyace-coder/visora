ABOUT VISORA

A home for
visual stories.

We are building an independent photography publishing platform designed to connect photographers, meaningful work and considered publishing opportunities.

OUR PURPOSE

Photography is more than an image.

Behind every photograph is a perspective, a moment and a person who made the decision to preserve it. Visora is being developed to give those photographs a thoughtful path from submission to potential acquisition and publication.

Our approach centres on considered review, clear permissions and proper photographer attribution. We want the relationship between the platform, the photographer and the work to be transparent.

COMPANY INFORMATION

Built with an international outlook.

Visora is being developed as an international photography publishing business, with the intention of establishing its operating company in the United Kingdom.

UK company registration is planned and has not yet been completed. The final registered legal name and company details will be published here once registration is complete.

THE VISORA APPROACH

Thoughtful work.
Clear relationships.

We believe photographers should understand how their work is reviewed, how permissions are handled and how their contributions are credited.
